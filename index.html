Quick Deliveries

Campus food delivery for Makerere University — order from your hall or lecture block, and get it delivered by a real person, guided there by turn-by-turn voice navigation.

Overview

Quick Deliveries is a Flutter and Firebase-based food ordering and delivery system built as a university project at Makerere University. Customers browse a menu and place an order in a couple of taps — no account required. Behind the scenes, an admin groups incoming orders by delivery time and hands each batch to a delivery guy, who gets a single "Start" button that opens live, voice-guided, traffic-aware navigation straight to the customer's location.

The system is built around three roles sharing one login screen — customer, admin, and delivery guy — with the experience shown to each person decided entirely by their account's role, not by which screen they happen to open.

Features

Guest-friendly ordering
Customers can browse the menu, add items to a cart, and place an order without ever creating an account — the app signs them in anonymously behind the scenes, so there's no signup friction between being hungry and getting food.

Automatic order grouping
Orders due around the same time are grouped into batches automatically, so the admin can hand a whole batch to one delivery guy at once instead of assigning orders one by one.

One-button delivery navigation
Once assigned, a delivery guy sees a single "Start" button per delivery. Tapping it opens a real, traffic-aware road route on Google Maps, with turn-by-turn directions spoken aloud — no need to read the screen while walking or riding.

Automatic arrival detection
There's no second "mark as delivered" button to remember — the moment the delivery guy's phone gets close enough to the destination, the order is marked delivered on its own.

Live weather and safety advisories
Before a delivery guy sets off, the app checks current weather conditions and speaks a warning aloud if it's raining, storming, or after dark — the information that actually matters to the person out on the road, not the person sitting at the base.

Live fleet tracking
The admin can see every delivery guy who's currently out on a delivery on one live map, updating as they move — with nothing shown for anyone who isn't actively delivering right now.

Built with accessibility in mind
Turn-by-turn directions, weather warnings, and arrival confirmations are all spoken aloud automatically, and key screens are labeled for screen readers — so the app remains usable even without being able to see it.

Built With

Flutter & Dart · Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) · Google Maps Platform (Maps SDK, Directions API, Distance Matrix API) · flutter_tts for on-device voice guidance
